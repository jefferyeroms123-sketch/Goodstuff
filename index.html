The Art of Lawn Maintenance While Pumping Water

Published in UNIBEN SUG Magazine | Category: Satirical Commentary

There is an undeniable, poetic elegance to campus infrastructure. Few experiences unite the undergraduate body quite like watching a pristine, freshly manicured lawn receive a lavish morning trim while three whole hostels organize a localized Olympic sprint for a single running tap.

"Priority is a matter of perspective. A dry bucket builds character, but unkempt grass? That damages institutional prestige."

As students carry yellow jerrycans with the posture of seasoned weightlifters, one must admire the commitment to aesthetics. The grass remains immaculate, trimmed with mathematical precision, standing as a testament to what can be achieved when administrative focus remains razor-sharp. Water utility management may be an ongoing chess match against municipal hydraulics, but the lawn care division operates with military discipline.

Academic Essay Sample

AI and Education in Nigeria

Undergraduate Competition Entry | Category: EdTech Analysis

I also write in more formal, structured registers when the work calls for it, here's an example from an academic competition.

The integration of artificial intelligence into the Nigerian educational landscape presents a paradox of access and capability. While global paradigms shift toward generative model assistance and automated assessment frameworks, local implementation faces foundational hurdles in infrastructure, bandwidth, and institutional readiness.

Bridging this gap requires moving beyond viewing AI as a replacement for traditional instruction. Instead, localized language models, offline-accessible tools, and targeted digital literacy programs offer a realistic roadmap for empowering students across tertiary institutions without relying on high-cost hardware setups.

Failed Zoologist: Special Grade House Flies

Format: Vertical Short Video Voiceover | Strategy: Anime Power-Scaling & Edutainment Hook

House flies are what happens when a character skips the whole training arc and goes straight to being overpowered.

From birth these guys are engineered to dodge anything. They have super fast reflexes, processing what happens around them so much faster than whatever your slow arm with a swatter can muster that it looks like slow motion to them. They really got Heavenly Restriction on insects now.🥀

Besides having a blessing from a god, they also have a whole innate technique dedicated to tasting. The moment they land, boom, that is it. Sugar, salt, whatever, your coffee's worth has been fully downloaded just by feet magic alone.

Then they got 360 degree vision, because why not. You can't even sneak up on them, they are always three steps ahead.

But just like every binding vow, they have their drawbacks. Our main character here can't take in solid food, so before they can get to touch anything, they gotta break it down and dissolve it with acid vomit. Yes, I kid you not, acid vomit. I guess even the strongest have to pay a cost for using their abilities.

So next time one dodges your swatter, don't take it personal. You're just fighting a special grade. Subscribe if you want more animals that are secretly built like anime characters.

Video Strategy & Retention Script

How to Make a Good Video

Format: Educational Video Essay / Scriptwriting Breakdown

Making a video that actually holds attention isn't about expensive cameras or fancy 3D transitions; it’s about managing dopamine drops and pacing.

Every successful piece of short-form or long-form content follows a core rule: total clarity within the first 3 seconds, continuous visual pattern interrupts every 2 to 4 seconds, and a payoff that fulfills the promise made in the title. If your visual pacing lags while your audio stays static, your audience swipes away. Great scripting bridges that gap by embedding visual cues directly into the narrative structure.
